Privacy Policy

Hinze & Co. · Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information our games handle and why. It applies to these games published by Hinze & Co.:

  • Snack Street

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (money, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

Our games show optional ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system, approximate location and how you interact with ads. Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

We do not receive this information ourselves beyond anonymous totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices in the game.
  • You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Watching ads is always optional; the games can be played without them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game, to delete everything it stored on your device. To ask Google about data it holds, see the Google Privacy Policy above.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
